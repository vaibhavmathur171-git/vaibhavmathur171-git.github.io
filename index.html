Magic Leap 2 · Spatial dimming

The Magic Leap 2 dimmer

The problem: AR glasses draw digital images on top of the real world. In bright sunlight, those images wash out.

The fix: darken only the part of the world behind the image. I invented the see-through screen that does this and led it from research to a shipped product.

MEMS tunable filters for medical imaging

A microscopic moving mirror (MEMS) sweeps a laser's color 200,000 times a second, letting a medical scanner (OCT) see beneath the surface of tissue. I did it end to end: design, simulation, fabrication, testing and production.
